What this is

SarcoMeta Explorer is the companion resource of a systematic review and meta-analysis of human skeletal muscle bulk RNA-seq. It pools 17 public cohorts (677 samples) into three contrasts:

  • Sarcopenia: sarcopenic or frail vs non-sarcopenic older adults (6 cohorts; 113 cases, 141 controls).
  • Ageing: healthy old vs young (7 cohorts; 199 old, 144 young).
  • Disuse in older adults: after vs before bed rest or reduced activity, paired (4 cohorts; 40 participants).

Results are compared with a meta-analysis of public mouse ageing and disuse datasets.

Methods in brief

Raw counts were uniformly re-processed (ARCHS4, or NCBI GEO raw counts where ARCHS4 lacked samples). Each cohort was analysed with limma-voom (TMM normalisation; paired designs for disuse; sex as a covariate where it varies). Genes were pooled by random-effects meta-analysis (REML). Between-condition differences use z = (b₁ − b₂)/√(se₁² + se₂²). Pathways were tested with fgsea on meta-analysis z-scores. The composition-adjusted z includes FibeRtypeR slow-fibre proportion and Kedlian-atlas estimates of FAPs, adipocytes, endothelial and myeloid cells as covariates. Mouse z values come from a meta-analysis of public mouse bulk RNA-seq using one-to-one orthologs.

An independent re-analysis from the raw files (new code; DESeq2 and limma-voom) reproduced the meta-analysis z-scores (Spearman ρ 0.97–1.00) and the main pathway findings.

Data and licence

All input data are public (NCBI GEO). The accessions are listed under Datasets. The summary results on this site may be reused with attribution.

Caveat

This is an exploratory resource accompanying a manuscript that has not yet been peer reviewed. Sarcopenia shows few individual genes at FDR < 0.05 and is best interpreted at pathway level. Interferon findings depend on single cohorts. Endothelial and immune composition estimates are method-dependent.
